### Features
- **Hero Section**: Welcoming area with clear value proposition
- **Troubleshooting Resources**: 4 cards covering common gaming issues
- **Performance Tools**: 9 curated optimization tools in 3 categories
- **Featured Articles**: 6 article cards on gaming optimization topics
- **Quick Tips**: 8 actionable performance tips with icons
- **Call-to-Action**: Engagement section with prominent button

- Blue-themed color palette (#0066CC, #1E88E5, #2196F3)

- Fully responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile
