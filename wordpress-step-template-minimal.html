Step-by-Step:

  • 1 Access your router's admin interface (e.g., 192.168.0.1 or tplinkwifi.net).
  • 2 Log in with your credentials.
  • 3 Navigate to NAT Forwarding > UPnP. Enable UPnP for automated port management.
  • 4 Alternatively, go to Virtual Servers/Port Forwarding to set manual rules based on game and platform (see below for port lists).
  • 5 Assign a static IP to your gaming device for consistency.
  • ✓ Save and reboot router and device as needed.
